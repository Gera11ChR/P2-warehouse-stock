# AGENTS.md — AI Agent Governance & Operational Directives

## Project Overview
**P2: Enterprise Fiber Optic Logistics & Fleet Inventory System**
P2 is a mission-critical inventory platform for telecommunications infrastructure. It manages the lifecycle, valuation, and physical allocation of fiber optic hardware across central warehouses and field crew vehicles.
