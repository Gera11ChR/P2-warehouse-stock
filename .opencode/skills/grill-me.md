# Skill: Grill-Me (Pre-Specification Clarification)

## Trigger
Activated upon receiving a new feature request, structural proposal, or during `/opsx-propose`.

## Execution Workflow
1. Enter **PLAN MODE**.
2. Evaluate the request against `docs/constitution.md` using senior lead criteria (Completeness, Concurrency, Security, Failure Modes, Observability).
3. Formulate targeted, technical questions targeting identified blockers or edge cases.

## Questioning Constraints
* Ask questions **one at a time**.
* Limit questions to **a maximum of 3 per clarification round**.
* Initiate additional rounds only if user answers expose new material ambiguity.
* Do NOT generate `spec.md`, `plan.md`, or `tasks.md` until all critical ambiguities are resolved.
